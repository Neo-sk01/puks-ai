Packaging Accounts Creation
Access:
• Packagings tab → Packaging Account
Screen Layout:
• Left panel: List of existing accounts showing:
o Account type (Internal or External)
o Account code
o Account designation
• Right panel (tabs vary by account type):
o External accounts:
▪ Packaging Types: Lists packaging types linked to the account,
their prices, and comments
▪ Third Parties: Lists third parties associated with the account
o Internal accounts:
▪ Activity: Shows activities linked to the account
Available Actions (Contextual Menu / Tabs)
For Accounts:
• Export to Excel
• Create new account
• Modify account
• Delete account
• View additional information
For Tabs (Packaging Types / Third Parties / Activity):
• Associate new item (Packaging Type, Third Party, or Activity)
• Edit
• Delete
• Print
Sensitivity: Internal

Creating an Account
1. Internal Account
• Click Add or New in the contextual menu
• Enter:
o Code
o Type: Internal
o Designation
o Contact details (optional)
o Comments (optional)
• Define activities:
o Go to Activity tab → New
o Select relevant activities from the list and validate
2. External Account
• Creation is similar to internal, but associations differ:
o Instead of activities, link:
▪ Types of Equipment (Packaging Types tab → New)
▪ Third Parties (Third Parties tab → New)
• Tick relevant items and validate
Important Notes
• Packaging management in SpeedWMS can only be fully executed after
configuring both packaging types and packaging accounts.
• Associations define how packaging is used in internal processes or linked to
external third parties.
Sensitivity: Internal

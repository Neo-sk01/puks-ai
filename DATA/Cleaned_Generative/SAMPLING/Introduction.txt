Topic: Introduction to Sampling in Quality Control
Purpose: Sampling is a traceability feature designed to manage and document
quality controls for received goods.
Objective: To systematically extract a sample from incoming shipments. This
sample is used to perform various quality inspections, which determine whether
the received items are approved for use in order picking and fulfillment.
Core Process Principle
1. Trigger: The process is initiated automatically after stock generation during
the receiving process.
2. Rule Application: The system evaluates and applies the pre-configured
sampling rules stored in the global system configuration.
3. Rule Outcome: These rules define three key elements for the sample:
o The quantity to be taken.
o The quality status to assign.
o The destination location for the sample (if specified).
4. Physical Execution: The original received handling unit (e.g., pallet, carton) is
split or "burst." The defined sample quantity is separated and set aside.
5. Quality Gate: The segregated sample then undergoes quality control. The
result of this inspection dictates the fate of the entire received batch:
o If compliant, the batch proceeds to active stock.
o If non-compliant, the batch is quarantined or rejected.

Loads management
Functionality accessible from the Shipping / Loading menu.
This feature allows to view all managed uploads.
The visualization of loads is divided into two configurable parts :
1.The upper part of the window displays the loading headers with their different
statuses (Created, In progress, Loaded, Shipped)
2.The lower part of the window allows you to view their content:
1.Affected orders/supports
2.Associated supports
By default the loadings are multi-activity. However, it is possible to deactivate the option
in the global settings of the Shipping in order to see only the loadings of the current
activity:

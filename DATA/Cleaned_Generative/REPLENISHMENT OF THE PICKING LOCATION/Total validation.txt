Total Validation
Purpose:
• To validate a replenishment line and update the system to reflect that the
goods have been processed and the associated support has been moved.
Access:
• Right-click on the Total Validation option after selecting a replenishment line
in the Replenishment window.
Effect:
• The selected replenishment line is validated.
• The corresponding support (pallet, bin, etc.) is moved in the system,
reflecting that the replenishment has been completed.
Sensitivity: Internal

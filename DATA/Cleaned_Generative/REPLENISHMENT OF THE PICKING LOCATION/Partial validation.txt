Topic: Partial Validation of a Replenishment
Access Path: Within the Replenishment Window, right-click on a
mission/replenishment line and select the context menu option: "Partially validate
the mission".
Purpose: To confirm the completion of only a portion of the total quantity requested
in a replenishment task.
Process Steps:
1. Enter Completed Quantity: In the validation window, manually enter the
quantity that has actually been replenished and moved to the target picking
location.
2. Manage Remaining Quantity:
o The system will automatically calculate the remaining quantity
(original requested quantity minus the validated quantity).
o Optional Modification: You can manually override this automatic
calculation. Click the edit icon (pen) next to the "Remaining qty" field
to directly enter or adjust the remaining balance.
Result: The replenishment task is updated. The validated quantity is closed, and a
new replenishment line is typically created for the remaining quantity to be
completed later.
Sensitivity: Internal

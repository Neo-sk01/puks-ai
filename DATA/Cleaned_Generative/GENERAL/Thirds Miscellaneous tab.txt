Thirds Miscellaneous Tab – RAG-Friendly Version
Purpose:
The Miscellaneous Tab allows associating additional, non-standard information
with a third party. This can include administrative, financial, or operational data
that is not captured in the main third-party fields.
1. Key Capabilities
Depending on the type of third party, the Miscellaneous tab can store:
• Supplier:
o VAT numbers, star codes, ownership information, or other
compliance details.
• Recipient:
o Special delivery instructions, account references, or other
operational notes.
• Carrier / Site:
o Loading/unloading locations, transport hub identifiers, or sitespecific information.
2. Usage Notes
• Fields in this tab are optional but recommended when additional
information is required for logistics, accounting, or legal compliance.
• Allows the system to capture extended attributes for a third party without
altering the standard tabs.
• Enhances reporting, validation, and integration with other modules such as
transport planning or stock management.

SpeedWMS – Reception Principle
Purpose:
Define the two methods of handling incoming goods (receptions) in SpeedWMS,
and explain how expected quantities are tracked and received.
1. Reception Methods
A. Portfolio of Expected Items
• Expected items correspond to arrival announcements or supplier purchase
orders.
• These can be entered:
o Manually by the user, or
o Automatically via an interface from an external system (e.g., N+1
sales management system).
• At the end of the reception process, SpeedWMS allows the generation of a
comparative report between:
o Quantities expected and
o Quantities actually received.
B. Reception Module
• Receptions are carried out declaratively, based on the items physically
received.
• Receptions can be fed by an interface or processed via:
o Radio Frequency (RF) module, or
o Manual entry.
2. Key Notes
• The choice of method depends on the business process and whether prenotification of arrivals is available.
• Comparative reporting in expected items ensures accurate tracking and
stock reconciliation.

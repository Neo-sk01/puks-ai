Comments Tab (Third Party)
Purpose
• Enables entering comments related to a third party (supplier, carrier,
recipient)
• These comments can then appear on preparation or delivery documents
Notes
• To have comments printed on documents, the printing status/template must
include a comments field
Essentially, this tab is used for document-level notes or instructions tied to a third
party, which can help during preparation or delivery processes.
Sensitivity: Internal

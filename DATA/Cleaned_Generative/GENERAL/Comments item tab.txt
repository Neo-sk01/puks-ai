Comments Tab (Third Party / Reception)
Purpose
• Enter comments related to a third party.
• These comments can appear on:
o Preparation slips
o Delivery slips
o Reception documents
Important Note
• To have comments printed, the corresponding report or document template
must include a comments field.
Essentially, this tab ensures custom notes for third-party operations are captured
and optionally printed on all relevant documents.
Sensitivity: Internal

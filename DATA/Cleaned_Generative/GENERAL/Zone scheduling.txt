Zone Scheduling – RAG-Friendly Version
Purpose:
Zone scheduling assigns a sequence number to each location within a zone,
establishing a hierarchy used for preparation slips, storage slips, or inventory lists.
This allows warehouse operations to follow a defined order of locations.
1. Key Concept
• Each location in a zone has a serial number.
• This sequence reflects the order in which slots were assigned to the zone.
• Adjustments may be needed when adding new locations or reassigning
existing ones.
2. Accessing Zone Scheduling
• Navigate to the list of locations assigned to a zone.
• Click the Order button (top-right of the list) to open the scheduling window.
3. Scheduling Options
a) Selection by Characteristics
• Locations can be filtered based on up to 5 characteristics (e.g., type, size,
activity).
• Both consecutive and non-consecutive locations can be selected.
b) Automatic Sorting
• Define priority order for up to 5 location characteristics.
• Choose ascending or descending for each characteristic.
• Use the arrows to:
o Change priority order.
o Reverse sort direction of a characteristic.
• Reverse order button reverses the order of all characteristics.
• Click Schedule to refresh the zone list with the new sort.
Sensitivity: Internal

c) Move or Reposition Slots
• Selected locations can be moved to a specific position in the list.
• Allows fine control over physical workflow or pick/put sequences.
d) Re-numbering Locations
• Assign a new starting sequence number for the first location.
• Remaining locations are numbered incrementally.
• Changes take effect after saving using the button at the bottom-left of the
window.
4. Notes
• Adjustments in sequence numbers impact picking, stock movements, and
inventory operations.
• Proper scheduling ensures optimized warehouse flows and minimizes errors
during order preparation or stock counting.

SpeedWMS – Receipt Generation from Expected Items
Purpose:
This operation allows the user to associate expected items with a receipt and
generate the corresponding receipt lines directly.
1. Scope of Operation
• Receipts are generated purchase order by purchase order.
• Even if multiple expected lines are selected, the entire purchase order is
returned to the processing screen.
• Processing multiple purchase orders at once is not supported; the operation
must be repeated for each purchase order.
• Accessible via the context menu on the expected items screen.
2. Receipt Line Population
• All lines from the purchase order are displayed on the screen.
• Editable fields are highlighted in yellow.
3. Managing Expected Quantities
• For each expected line:
1. Check the quantity to be returned to stock.
2. Modify quantity if required.
3. Tick the TG (“To Generate”) box to mark the line for processing in the
receipt.
• Lines already received are automatically ticked as G (“Generated”) and
cannot be modified.
• Variable weight items: specify the total weight of the expected quantity.
• Partial receipt: If a quantity lower than expected is received, the line will be
split, keeping a remainder for future processing.
4. Receipt Validation
Sensitivity: Internal

• Click the Validation button once all lines are reviewed and adjusted.
• The system will present different validation options based on the
configuration.
5. Inheritance of Additional Information
• If additional information was saved on the expected items:
o And inheritance or inheritance mapping is activated,
o These details will appear automatically in the generated receipt.
6. Notes
• After receipt generation, proceed to stock generation and further processing
as per the standard reception workflow.
• Refer to the Stock Generation SOP for handling stock movements and
updates.

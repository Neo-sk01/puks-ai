Ribbon – My Shortcuts Section:
Contains three menus to manage favourites and shortcuts:
1. Widget Menu – Manage widgets inserted in the dynamic menu.
2. Personal Menu – Configure user-specific shortcuts and preferences.
3. Add a Shortcut – Create direct access to frequently used operational
functions.

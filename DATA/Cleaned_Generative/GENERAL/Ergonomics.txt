SpeedWMS Ergonomics & User-Friendliness
SpeedWMS is designed with ergonomics and ease of use as core principles. Users
can:
1. Display & Personalization
• Configure which information is shown on list screens.
• Customize column titles for clarity.
2. Printing & Editions
• Select from a list of available document editions.
• Create activity-specific editions if required.
• Modify personal edition templates (for trained users).
3. Filtering & Queries
• Apply filters to screens to quickly find information.
• Build custom queries for specific operational needs.
4. Data Export & Import
• Export screen content to Excel, XML, or Word.
• Import data from Excel or CSV files.
5. Communication
• Send emails directly from the interface.
• Initiate phone calls to contacts from screens (e.g., receipts, order entry).
Essentially, SpeedWMS empowers users to adapt screens, reports, and
interactions to their workflows, making operations faster and reducing errors.

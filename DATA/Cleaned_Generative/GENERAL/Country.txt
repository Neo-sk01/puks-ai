Country Management
Access
• Menu: Settings → Third p. → Country
Purpose
• Mainly used for transport management
• Allows configuring and declaring a list of countries in SpeedWMS
Available Actions
1. Create new country codes
2. Modify existing country properties
3. Delete existing country codes (subject to constraints)
Country Form Fields
Field Description
Country code Unique identifier for the country, alphanumeric, max 6 characters
Country name Full name of the country, max 50 characters
Language Country language code, max 5 characters
Belongs to EU Checkbox to indicate if the country is part of the European Union
• Notes:
o The Language and EU membership fields can influence:
▪ Automatic document printing language
▪ Type of document to print based on whether the country
belongs to the EU or not
This is a simple but essential configuration for transport and document automation
in SpeedWMS.
Sensitivity: Internal

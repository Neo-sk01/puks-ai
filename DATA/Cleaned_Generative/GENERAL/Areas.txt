Warehouse Areas / Zones – RAG-Friendly Version
Purpose:
Zones in Speed WMS group warehouse locations to facilitate operations such as
reception, storage, picking, preparation, and inventory. Each zone type has a
specific role in warehouse management. Zones are multi-activity, meaning a zone
created in the environment is available for all activities an operator may access.

2. Notes
• Non-exclusive zones: A location can belong to multiple zones of the same
type.
• Exclusive zones: A location can only belong to one zone of that type.
• Zones are reusable across activities, making configuration flexible and
efficient.

Declarative Storage – Assigning Planned Locations
Access:
• Open a Reception form → Use the Assign option from the popup/context
menu.
Purpose
• Assign a destination location for each support manually.
• Supports can then be prepared for storage movements and later used for
order picking.
Steps
1. Open the reception form.
2. Select a line (support) → choose Assign from the contextual menu.
3. Fill in the Planned location field for each support:
o Enter the location name manually, or
o Search and select via the Assign the planned location option.
Outcome
• As soon as a destination location is assigned, the support’s status changes
to 03 (“movement to be performed”).
• This indicates the support has a known planned location and is ready for
storage processing.
Sensitivity: Internal

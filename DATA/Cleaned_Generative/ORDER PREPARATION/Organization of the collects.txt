Organization of the Collects
Purpose
• To manage and prioritize the sequence in which pick-ups (collect missions)
are prepared.
Access
• Through the context menu in the Collects window → select Order Collects.
Functionality
• Select a pick-up mission line.
• Use the up/down arrows to move the line in the list.
• This changes the priority of the pick-ups.
• The order number of the collect mission updates automatically to reflect the
new sequence.
Sensitivity: Internal

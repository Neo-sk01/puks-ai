Creating a Distribution
Access:
Preparation / Distribution
• Orders must be in In Seizure (010) status to create a distribution.
• Click the Addition (+) button to open the distribution form.
• The form is divided into several tabs.
Header Tab
Displayed by default, divided into three main sections:
Section Purpose
Select a distribution template and enter initialization info for the
Distribution
distribution.
External
Enter information related to the third-party supplier.
Data
Organization Fill in delay information and distribution preparation circuit.
Lines Tab
• Displays items from the selected distribution template.
• Adjustments via contextual menu:
o Add new lines using the addition button.
o Only items for which the selected supplier is the main supplier can be
selected.
• Mandatory input per line:
o Quantity to allocate
o Weight
o Traceability
• Distribution methods:
o Entered Distribution: Manually enter quantities per line based on
assigned percentages.
Sensitivity: Internal

[PAGE 2]
o Auto Distribution: SpeedWMS automatically fills line quantities based
on the distribution template.
Recipient Lines Tab
• Displays how the parcels are distributed per recipient.
• Allows modification of the number of parcels assigned to each recipient.
Comments Tab
• Enter comments that will appear in the distribution-related documents.
General Information Tab
• Tracks creation and updates of the distribution.
Additional Info Tab
• Optional information can be added at different levels of the process:
o Division
o Preparation voucher (BP)
o Delivery note (BL)
o Label
Info Tab
• Summary of general information of the current distribution.
Key Notes
• A distribution is linked to a template, a supplier, and specific recipients.
• Quantities can be assigned manually or automatically.
• All tabs allow for documentation and traceability of the distribution process.
Sensitivity: Internal

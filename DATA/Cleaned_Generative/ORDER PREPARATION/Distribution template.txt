Distribution Templates
Access
• Menu: Preparation / Distribution Templates
Overview
• Displays a list of existing templates.
• Allows you to create, modify, or delete templates.
Creating a New Template
1. Click New to open the creation window.
2. Enter the following details:
o Template Name: Name for the template.
o Code: Select the third-party recipients for the order.
o Percentage: Define the percentage of goods to be distributed to each
recipient.
3. Click Validate to finalize the creation.
Managing Templates
• Newly created templates are added to the list of existing templates.
• Templates can be modified or removed using the corresponding buttons.
Tip: Distribution templates help automate the splitting of goods among
recipients according to predefined percentages, reducing manual workload during
order processing.

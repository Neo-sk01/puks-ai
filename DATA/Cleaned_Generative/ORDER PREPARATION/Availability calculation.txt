Availability Calculation Overview
Purpose:
• Optional step before launching orders in preparation.
• Determines whether selected orders can be fully served based on current
stock availability.
• Helps identify if restocking a preparation area is necessary before picking.
Access:
• Configured in SpeedWMS for selected orders prior to launch.
Types of Availability Rates
1. Absolute Rate
o Calculated for the entire warehouse.
o Provides an overall availability of items.
o Considers the total need of the orders.
2. Proximity Rate
o Calculated for an advanced preparation zone.
o Determines if goods transfers to this zone are required.
o Total need is taken into account.
3. Retail Rate
o Calculated for areas where retail withdrawals occur.
o Only considers the requirement outside full pallets.
o Useful for small-quantity or picking operations.
Key Notes
• Not all three rates need to be configured; only the required rate can be set.
• Provides a pre-launch check to optimize stock allocation and reduce
preparation delays.
• The calculated rates can influence launch decisions, especially if stock is
insufficient.

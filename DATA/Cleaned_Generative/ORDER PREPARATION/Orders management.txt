Orders Management
Access
• Menu: Preparation → Orders
Purpose
• Provides a portfolio of all orders in SpeedWMS.
• Orders are organized into tabs based on their status.
• Default filter: Creation date in a specific viewing period.
• Filters can be customized by:
o Creation date
o Preparation date
o Delivery date
o Printing date
o Wave release date
o Modification date

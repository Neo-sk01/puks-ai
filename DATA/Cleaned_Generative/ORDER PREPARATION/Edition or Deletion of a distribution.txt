Edition / Deletion of a Distribution
Access
• Menu: Preparation / Distribution
• Options: Available in the contextual menu under General Actions
Key Points
• Edit Distribution: Allows modification of a distribution before it is launched.
• Delete Distribution: Allows removal of a distribution before it is launched.
• Restriction: Once a distribution has been launched, it cannot be edited or
deleted.
Tip: Always ensure that the distribution details are correct before launching, as
changes are irreversible afterward.
Sensitivity: Internal

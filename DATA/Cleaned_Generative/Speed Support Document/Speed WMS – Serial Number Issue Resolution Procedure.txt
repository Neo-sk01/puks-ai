Procedure: Resolving Serial Number Issues in Speed (e.g., INC0379425)
1. Objective
To resolve incidents where serial numbers are not associating correctly in Speed,
even though they appear in reports (e.g., SSRS), using database verification and
updates.
2. Example Incident
Incident: INC0379425 – Skyworth Durban
Issue:
• 100 serial numbers for 2 pallets (PSD000977 and PSD000984) were not
associating in Speed.
• Serial numbers appeared correctly in the SSRS serial number report, but not in
Speed.
Root Cause:
• Spaces or special characters (e.g., line breaks) were present in the serial number
table (NSE_DAT).
• Speed does not accept special characters in serial number fields.
3. Steps to Resolve
Step 1 – Verify Data in Database
Check serial number records in the production Speed database:
SELECT NSE_SSCCC, *
FROM nse_dat
WHERE NSE_SSCCC LIKE '%PSD000984%';
• nse_dat: Serial number table
• NSE_SSCCC: Field representing pallet number
Step 2 – Identify the Issue
Sensitivity: Internal

[PAGE 2]
• Look for spaces, line breaks, or special characters in the NSE_SSCCC field.
• Confirm the affected records correspond to the incident pallets.
Step 3 – Fix the Issue
Remove unwanted characters using the REPLACE function:
UPDATE nse_dat
SET NSE_SSCCC = REPLACE(NSE_SSCCC, CHAR(10), '')
WHERE act_code = 'RSADBNWH'
AND art_code IN ('SKY-HS6409', 'SKY-R012000030', 'SKY-R012000029');
• CHAR(10) represents a line break or special character
• Filter by ACT_CODE and ART_CODE to update only relevant records
Step 4 – Validate the Fix
1. Re-run the SELECT query from Step 1 to confirm that special characters are
removed.
2. Check with operations that the serial numbers now associate correctly in
Speed.
4. Notes
• Always verify the data before applying updates to avoid impacting unrelated
records.
• Maintain an audit log of:
o Pallet/serial numbers updated
o Database changes made
o Date/time and operator
• This procedure can be reused for any serial number issue caused by unwanted
characters.

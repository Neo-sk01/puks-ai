Receiving tab
The tab is divided in two sub-tabs, allowing to act on the default qualities to affect to the
item, as well as on the behaviour of the receiving radio module.
Possibility to assign the default quality of the item for any receipt o return. If no quality is
provided, the default quality of the activity will be applied.
It is also possible to specify the default owner of the item for any receipt or return. The
system lists all the third known as “owner”. If no owner is specified, the default owner of
the activity will be considered as the owner of the item.
Allows you to modify the behaviour of the receiving radio module regarding the allowed
SU being seized by the operators.
Sensitivity: Internal.The list on the left is used to configure the input fields in which the operators can enter
a quantity.
.The list on the right is used to define the field that will be active by default when the
opening of the quantity entry screen.

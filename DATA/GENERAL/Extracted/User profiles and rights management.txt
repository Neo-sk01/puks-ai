User profiles and rights management
Each user must have a profile in order to access SpeedWMS and its various modules.
A user must be attached to a group in which the various access rights are configured.
The user therefore inherits the rights of the group. Depending on the rights desired, it
may therefore be necessary to create new groups, to configure the associated rights
before being able to associate the various user accounts with them.
The management of rights and profiles is available via the menu System / Rights :

Quality
Functionality accessible from the menu Setting / Items / Quality
The quality enables to define the different supports being in stock , in order to manage
the nature of the goods (e.g.: standard, non-compliant, Anomaly, Litigation, etc.). Each
stock line will have a quality assigned (STD by default) when the input process starts
(receiving).
The quality widow displays the list of the quality codes saved in SpeedWMS:
It is possible to :
.Create new quality codes
.Modify the properties of the existing quality (only the description can be modified with
this option)
.Delete (not without certain constraints) an existing quality
The quality sheet opens as soon as a creation or modification action is initiated:
Sensitivity: Internal.Activity: non editable element that corresponds to the activity for which the quality
was created and managed.
.Quality code: unique identifier of the quality. Maybe an alphanumeric (5 characters
maximum).
.Description : quality description (50 characters maximum)
Sensitivity: Internal

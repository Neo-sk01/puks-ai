Ergonomics
Ergonomics and ease of use are the fundamental principles that have guided the design
of SpeedWMS.
In a very simple way, it is possible for users to:
.Set up the information that can be displayed on the list screens, as well as their title
.Choose an edition from a list of available editions (by default the printing statuses are
common, but it is possible to create specific statuses by activity, displayed only for the
said activity)
.Modify the model of its own editions (users trained in mock-up)
.Use filters and build your own queries
.Export the content of many screens to Excel, Xml and Word
.Import data from Excel or any CSV
.Send an email directly, or initiate a phone call to a contact in the entry screens
(receipts, order entry, etc.)

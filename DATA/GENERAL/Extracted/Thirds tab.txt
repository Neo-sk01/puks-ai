Thirds tab
Tab used to link the item sheet to other Third Parties than the one that has been defined
as the main supplier.
The main supplier of the reference will be present by default in the list.
The options available on the contextual menu will allow, among other things, to add an
assignment to another third party for which it will be possible, for example, to define the
code, the label and the identifier under which this article is referenced for this third
party.
Sensitivity: Internal

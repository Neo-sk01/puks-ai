Country
Functionality accessible from the menu Settings/ Third p. /Country.
Mainly used for transport management, this functionality enables to configure and
declare a list of countries in SpeedWMS
It will then be possible to :
.Create new country codes
.Modify the properties of an existing country
.Delete (not without certain constraints) an existing country

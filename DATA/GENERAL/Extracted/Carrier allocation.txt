Carrier allocation
Functionality available from the menu Settings / Transport / Carrier Allocation
The default screen (Sequences tab) of this feature enables to set the sequences for the
assignment of the orders with the right carrier code.
The setting of the sequences can be based on all the headings of the tables linked to the
existing orders in SpeedWMS.
It is necessary to activate a heading in the Columns tab before being able to position a
condition. The user just need to check any line necessary for the construction of the
different sequences :
Once the headings selected, it will be possible to create news sequences by the using
the addition button or through the contextual menu :
Sensitivity: InternalThe sequence will enable the allocation of a specified carrier any time the conditions
are fulfilled. Depending on the need, it is not necessary to fill in all the conditions :
The sequences can all be modified or reordered tanks to the arrows on the upper right
corner of the window
The carrier assignment also be realized directly from the orders window (see
corresponding paragraph).

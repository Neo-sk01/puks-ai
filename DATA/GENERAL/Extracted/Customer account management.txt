Customer account management
Functionality accessible only if the activity manages the Customer account.
It enables to split the activity by customer account and each receiving or preparation
operation will necessarily be assigned to a customer account.
Customer account management must be activated in the relevant activity sheet.
Access: System / Activity / double-click on the activity:
It will be possible to define if the data (items, third parties, etc.) are common to all
customer accounts.
Those which are not will see their pr code prefixed with the relevant customer account.
.Create different customer accounts
.Validate and restart SpeedWMS for the change to be taken into account.

.Designation :customer account name.
Sensitivity: Internal.Prefix :alphanumeric characters (maximum of 5 characters). The account item codes
will consist of the defined prefix followed by the item code.
Possibility to also assign a logo to the customer account
Then you have to fill in the coordinates are of the customer; it is even possible to add
useful Comments that might give information when preparing an order, generating a

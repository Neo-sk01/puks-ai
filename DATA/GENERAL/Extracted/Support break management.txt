Support break management
The support break is a parameter specific to each item form, used when constructing
receipt lines. Depending on the configuration of the item sheet, it will for example be
possible to create 5 different storage media in a single administrative entry.
This parameter is positioned in each item sheet, in the Storage tab :
The stock units (SU) that can be activated as a support break criterion will directly
depend on the SU activated on the item sheet in question under the features:

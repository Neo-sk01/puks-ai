Principle
Receptions can be handled in two different ways under Speed:
-from a portfolio of expected items : the expected correspond to arrival
announcements, or to supplier purchase orders which are, in this case, entered
manually or directly interfaced, possibly from the n + 1 sales management system. It is
then possible, at the end of reception, to obtain a comparative report between the
quantities which were expected and those which have actually been received.
-from the reception module : the reception is carried out in a declarative manner, on
the basis of what will actually be.
In this case, an interface can feed the reception; the reception itself can then be done
using a radio frequency module (See RF doc) or by manual entry.
Sensitivity: Internal

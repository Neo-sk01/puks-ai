Launch in Preparation – Overview
The launch in preparation is the process that takes orders from entry and assigns
stock to order lines, generating preparation missions and eventually preparation
notes (PNs).
Key Principles
1. Execution Scope
o Can be executed for one or more orders simultaneously.
o Each order line is processed individually based on stock availability
and service rules.
2. Missions
o A mission groups samples (stock assignments) created during launch.
o One mission can include multiple orders.
o One order can be split across multiple missions.
o Missions form the basis for preparation notes (PN).
Process Flow per Order Line
For each order line, SpeedWMS will:
1. Find procedures for sample creation
o These are the pre-configured rules for generating stock allocations.
2. Check application conditions
o Activity, recipient, item, and other filters defined in the procedure.
o Only applicable order lines are processed.
3. Apply stock search conditions
o Check zones, SU (stock units), and other stock parameters.
o Only unblocked stock is considered.
4. Create samples
o Each sample is an assignment of stock to an order line, including:

▪ SU (stock unit)
▪ Quality
▪ Location
▪ Quantity
o Sample types:
1. Sample from stock – Direct allocation from stock.
2. Picking sample – Allocation from the picking location.
3. Missing sample – When stock is insufficient.
5. Ensure fulfillment
o Multiple samples may be created until the sum of all samples = order
line quantity.
Mission Generation Rules
• The portfolio of created samples is analyzed to generate missions.
• Rules are applied sequentially and are defined by application criteria such
as:
o Activity
o Zone
o Type of sample
o Reason
o Recipient
• Generation parameters determine:
o How withdrawals are sorted.
o Break rules that trigger the creation of a mission.
Each sample must belong to a mission before the process is considered complete.

Assignment of a Collect
Purpose:
• Assign a collect mission to a specific operator for processing in the
warehouse.
Access:
• Contextual menu in the Generated or In progress tabs of the Collect window.
• Option: Assign to…
Process Steps
1. Open Assignment Window
o Selecting Assign to… opens a window listing all users registered in
SpeedWMS.
2. Select Operator
o Choose the operator who will be responsible for processing the
collect.
o Multiple collects can be assigned to the same operator using multiple
selection.
3. Confirm Assignment
o The selected collect(s) are now assigned to the chosen operator.
4. Unassign if Needed
o If an error occurs or the operator is unavailable, the collect can be
unassigned using the Deallocate option in the context menu.
Key Notes
• Ensures that warehouse staff have clear responsibilities for pick-up
missions.
• Supports multiple assignments and corrections (unassigning) for flexibility
in operations.
Sensitivity: Internal

Other Order Information (Appendix Tabs)
Beyond the header and line details, SpeedWMS allows you to manage additional
information for each order using appendix tabs.
Comments Tab
• Purpose: Record comments related to the order.
• Output: Comments will appear on:
o Preparation slips
o Delivery slips
Wave Tab
• Purpose: View the preparation wave the order belongs to.
• Notes:
o Orders in the entry/input stage are not yet attached to any wave.
o Once in a wave, the tab shows wave details for tracking.
Footer Tab
• Purpose: Displays quantitative information of the order, including:
o Quantities ordered
o Weights
o Volumes
• Dynamic update: These fields update automatically as the preparation and
shipping processes are executed.
Info Tab
• Purpose: Provides load-related information for the order.
• Details:
o Load(s) the order may be attached to
Sensitivity: Internal

[PAGE 2]
o List of media prepared for the order and expected on the load
Additional Info Tab
• Purpose: Hold extra information about the selected order line.
• Flexibility: Can be used for special notes or internal references not covered
in other tabs.
Tip: These tabs allow you to centralize all relevant order details, making
tracking, reporting, and wave management easier.
Sensitivity: Internal

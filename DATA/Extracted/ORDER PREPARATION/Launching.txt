Launching a Distribution
Access:
Preparation / Distribution
Steps to Launch a Distribution
1. Select the distribution(s) you want to process.
2. Use the contextual menu and choose:
GO: Validate the distributions
What Happens When You Launch
• Orders are generated based on the distribution model.
• Orders are automatically validated (depending on configuration).
• Picking notes and labels are created for the orders.
• Optional: Print a label per recipient or per shipping support using the Print
markup labels option.
Notes
• Launching a distribution is the step that converts received goods into
prepared orders.
• Ensures that the distribution is ready for picking and shipping.
• Labels can be customized per recipient for easier handling.

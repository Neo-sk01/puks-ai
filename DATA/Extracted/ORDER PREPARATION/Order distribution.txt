Order Distribution
This function automatically distributes received goods to different customers
according to predefined models and can transform received products into
prepared products immediately.
Steps in the Distribution Process
Step 1: Creation of Models
• Define recipient codes (customers).
• Specify distribution percentages for each recipient.
• If percentages aren’t fixed, the model can be created with 0%, and
percentages can be entered later after importing data.
Step 2: Creation of the Distribution Header
• Import the distribution model.
• Enter the supplier code for the received goods.
Step 3: Entry of Lines to be Received
• Input the items and quantities to be received, as usual in receipt entry.
Step 4: Generation of the Distribution
• Validation triggers the automatic actions:
1. Stock creation – the received goods are added to stock.
2. Creation of different orders – based on the distribution model.
3. Validation of the different orders – automatically confirms them.
4. Edition of validation notes – prepares documents for reference.
Note: You can configure the system to not automatically validate orders, if needed.
Key Points:
• Distribution can be automatic and immediate, turning receipts into prepared
products.
• Percentages can be preset or updated post-import.
Sensitivity: Internal

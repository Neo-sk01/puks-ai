Sampling details
Functionality available from the Shipping menu / Sampling.
This feature allows you to view all the picking in progress, classify them and assign them
various shipping items. It is used for maintenance operations, and not recommended
for operational processes.
In particular, it allows you to control:
.The content of an order, a mission, a support etc.
.The status of an order, a mission, a support etc.
These direct debits have a changing status, corresponding to the different tabs available
in the window:
.To prepare: the samples are generated and are to be prepared; status 010
.Prepared: samples are taken. The stock issue has been carried out; status 040
.Pre-shipped: the samples are packed; status 060
.Shipped: samples are shipped; status 070
.Terminated: direct debits are balanced; status 050
Sensitivity: Internal

Workshop Zone Settings
Access
• Menu: Settings → Locations → Areas
• Used to define workshop zones and assign them to production locations for
manufacturing.
Workshop Zone Definition
• A workshop zone is a defined area where manufacturing activities occur.
• Once created, locations must be assigned to this zone (via Zone
Management).
• These assigned locations appear in the right side of the screen for reference.
Approach Settings
These parameters determine how components are approached (moved) to the
workshop before manufacturing begins.
Key Configuration Options
1. Manufacturing Location
o The location where production is physically carried out.
o Only one location per workshop area can be defined as the
manufacturing location.
2. Replenishment Methods
o Transfer by Support
▪ Moves a complete support (full pallet) for production.
▪ Configuration options:
▪ Origin stock area: area where components are picked
from.
▪ Search rule: defines where components are moved
within the workshop.
▪ Selected item family: specifies which items are
affected.
Sensitivity: Internal

[PAGE 2]
▪ Horizon (Nb days): number of days in the MO portfolio
considered for quantity calculation.
o Pick-List
▪ Generates a transfer order for components matching the MO
requirements (rounded to the configured Stock Unit).
▪ Configuration options:
▪ Recipient: internal order destination (the workshop
location).
▪ Preparation channel: defines the channel used for the
generated order.
▪ Preparation type: must be Replenishment.
▪ Approach time (Nb days): initializes the preparation and
delivery dates for the order.
Purpose
• Ensures that all required components are available in the workshop before
production starts.
• Configures whether full pallets or quantities per pick-list are used for
replenishment.
• Integrates with MO portfolio horizon to calculate the required quantities in
advance.
Sensitivity: Internal

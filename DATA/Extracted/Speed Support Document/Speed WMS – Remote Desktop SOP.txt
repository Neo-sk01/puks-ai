1. SCOPE
This procedure is applicable to the WMS department, situated at 20 Maple Street Pomona Kempton Park,
which is managed and operated by Africa Global Logistics in South Africa.
2. PURPOSE
The purpose of this standard operating procedure (SOP) is to provide a step-by-step instruction of logging
on in the Remote Desktop Manager. The goal is to provide a high level of service and quality to achieve
efficiency and uniformity in all operational activities. This is to ensure that relevant staff personnel know
and understand what is required.

4. PROCESS
The remote desktop includes various activities that determine the way goods/system/Interfaces are
processed/managed/resolved in the warehouse management systems department.
The Remote Desktop flow comprises of the following default processes:
• Logging onto MSC Red account
• Checking Out
• Generating a Password and length of Session
• Logging into Servers
5. GENERATING PASSWORD ON RED ACCOUNT
1. Select the drop-down menu on the search bar ‘Search within’.
2. Select the ‘My Tiered Red Accounts’ option.
3. Click the Search icon to refresh the page.
4. Once refreshed, click onto the account name displayed under ‘Display Name’ e.g. “Name Surname (RED
Account) (Tier- 1 Account)”
5. Click on the ‘Check-out’ tab.
6. Tick the box for ‘Check-out my password’.
7. Select the drop down for ‘Session valid for’ and select the desired timeline. E.g. “9 hours from now”
8. Click ‘OK’ on the bottom left of the window.
9. Then click ‘Submit’ again to confirm your request. The page will automatically refresh upon
confirmation when you click ‘Submit’.
10. Repeat Steps 4 & 5 again.
11. Copy the password from ‘Current Password’
6. LOGGING INTO REMOTE DESKTOP MANAGER
1. Once on the Remote Desktop. Open ‘Remote Desktop Manager’.
2. Under the ‘Navigation’ Tab, right-click on the rdm folder. (Figure 1)
3. Select Properties on the Pop-up. (Figure 2)

8. SELECTING A SERVER
Double Click on the desired server.
9. SIGNING OUT OF THE SERVER
1. Click the ‘Windows’ Button or select ‘Windows’ on the left corner of the Taskbar.
2. Click on your Profile.
3. Click ‘Sign out’
10. ADDITIONAL INFORMATION (E.G. LOCKED ACCOUNT)
In an instance where you get an error message that says “account locked because there were too many
incorrect logons”
You can follow the necessary steps to Unlock your account.
1. Select the ‘My Tiered Red Accounts’ option.
2. Click the Search icon to refresh the page.
3. Once refreshed, click onto the account name displayed under ‘Display Name’ e.g. “Name Surname (RED
Account) (Tier- 1 Account)”
4. Click on the ‘Reset Password/Unlock Account’ tab.
5. Tick the box for ‘Unlock this Account in AD?’
6. Click ‘OK’ on the bottom left of the window.
7. Then click ‘Submit’ again to confirm your request. The page will automatically refresh upon
confirmation when you click ‘Submit’.

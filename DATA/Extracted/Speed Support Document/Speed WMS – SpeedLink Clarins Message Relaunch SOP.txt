Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
Title: Relaunching Messages for Clarins via SpeedLink
Department: WMS JACC
Effective Date: 23/06/2025
Prepared By: Waseela Salim
1. Objective
To guide team members through relaunching Clarins messages in SpeedLink on the
Production Server (PFS03 GMT +2) when a relaunch request is received.
2. Procedure
Step 1 – Connect to the Production Server
1. Launch your remote desktop or server access tool.
2. Connect to the Production Server: PFS03 GMT +2.
3. Log in using authorized credentials.
Step 2 – Access the SpeedLink Directory
1. Open File Explorer.
2. Navigate to:
F:\Interfaces\P-ZAF-CA-S1\SpeedLink
3. Locate and open the SpeedLink application.
Step 3 – Log Into SpeedLink
1. On the login screen, enter:
o Access Code: LINKUP
o Password: 1234
2. Press Enter.
3. You will be redirected to the SpeedLink Homepage.
Sensitivity: Internal

[PAGE 2]
Step 4 – Access the Markings Section
1. On the left-hand navigation panel, click Markings.
2. The Markings screen will appear.
Step 5 – Filter for the Message
1. Use the Order_Key field to filter for the specific order.
2. Select the message type to be relaunched from the drop-down menu.
3. Click Refresh to update the view.
Step 6 – Remove the Marking
1. Once the message appears:
o Right-click on the message.
o Click Remove marking from the context menu.
o Confirm in the pop-up by clicking Yes.
2. The message will now be queued for the next scheduled run.
Step 7 – Verify Message Relaunch
1. Navigate to the Historical section via the navigation panel.
2. Click Refresh until the next scheduled run completes.
3. Confirm that the relaunched message appears in the Historical logs.
Step 8 – Final Confirmation
1. Notify Clarins and confirm whether the message was successfully received.
3. Important Notes
• Only remove markings for messages explicitly requested by Clarins.
• Follow all steps precisely to avoid processing errors.
Sensitivity: Internal

[PAGE 3]
• In case of any errors, escalate immediately to your supervisor or the support
team.

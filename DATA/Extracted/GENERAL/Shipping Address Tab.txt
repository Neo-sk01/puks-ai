Shipping Address Tab – RAG-Friendly Version
Purpose:
The Shipping Address Tab stores contact and address information for a third party.
It enables the management of multiple addresses beyond the primary one, such as
additional delivery addresses or billing addresses.
1. Key Capabilities
• Maintain multiple addresses for a single third party.
• Use addresses for:
o Document generation (delivery notes, invoices, etc.)
o Order preparation and logistics operations
• Quick access to the address portfolio via the envelope icon in SpeedWMS.
2. Usage Notes
• Each address entry can include standard contact details (street, city, postal
code, etc.) and can be designated for specific functions (delivery, billing,
etc.).
• Ensures accurate routing of shipments and correspondence.
• Integrates with other SpeedWMS modules, supporting automation in order
handling and reporting.

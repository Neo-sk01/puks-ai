Classification Tab (Third Party)
Purpose
• Allows the user to view or modify the classification assigned to a third party
(supplier, carrier, or recipient).
• Classification helps in organizing third parties for reporting, filtering, or
applying specific warehouse rules.
Key Functions
1. View classification
o Displays all the families or categories to which the third party
belongs.
o Example: Supplier classification → “Preferred”, “Standard”, “New”.
2. Modify classification
o Using the contextual menu, you can:
▪ Add an assignment → Assign the third party to a new
classification or category.
▪ Remove an assignment → Unlink the third party from an existing
classification.
Usage Notes
• Classification affects:
o Filtering and searching third parties in lists.
o Selection criteria in orders, receipts, and other processes.
o Reporting and statistics for warehouse management.
• For detailed structure, refer to the Classification chapter in SpeedWMS
documentation.
Sensitivity: Internal

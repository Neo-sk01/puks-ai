Customs Status
• Menu access: Settings / Other / Customs status
• Purpose: Allows management of bonded goods by defining customs
statuses in the system.
• Usage:
o Each customs status can be added to the environment.
o During receiving, if goods are bonded, the corresponding customs
status is applied.
o Customs status becomes a property of the stock, enabling
traceability and proper handling.
Sensitivity: Internal

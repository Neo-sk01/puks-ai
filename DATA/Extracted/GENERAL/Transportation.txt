Transportation – RAG-Friendly Version
Purpose:
Define and manage the transportation-related elements of goods within
SpeedWMS, ensuring smooth logistics, planning, and tracking across warehouse
activities.
1. Overview
• The Transportation module is used to configure and manage all aspects of
goods movement between locations, warehouses, or external sites.
• It supports cross-functional logistics, integrating with receiving, storage,
order preparation, and shipment processes.
2. Key Functions
1. Transport Definition
o Create and configure transport methods, vehicles, and carriers.
o Assign transport types to shipments or orders.
2. Planning & Scheduling
o Define planned routes and delivery schedules.
o Coordinate with warehouse operations to ensure availability of goods
for transport.
3. Tracking & Status Management
o Monitor the status of goods in transit.
o Link transport operations to related SpeedWMS receipts, orders, or
missions for visibility.
4. Integration with Warehouse Processes
o Transportation settings impact stock movements, order preparation,
and shipment execution.
o Supports RF scanning, stock verification, and real-time updates.
3. Summary
Sensitivity: Internal

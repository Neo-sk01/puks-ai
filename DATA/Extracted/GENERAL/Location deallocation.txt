SpeedWMS – Location Deallocation
Purpose:
• Allows the removal of locations from a zone at any time.
• Useful when reorganizing warehouse zones or updating location
assignments.
Steps to Deallocate Locations:
1. Select the location(s) to be removed from the zone.
2. Right-click on the selection.
3. Choose “Deallocate location” from the contextual menu.
The selected locations will be removed from the assigned zone immediately.
Notes:
• This operation only affects the zone assignment, not the location itself.
• Deallocated locations can be reassigned later to the same or a different
zone if needed.
Sensitivity: Internal

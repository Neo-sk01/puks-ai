SpeedWMS Functional Coverage
SpeedWMS manages warehouse operations at the location level, supporting both
simple and complex processes, including multi-activity management, burst
preparation, and radio frequency setup.
1. Warehouse Topography
• Management by Location: All types of locations, different area types (dock,
reception, preparation, inventory, etc.).
• Stock Visualization: Classic display of stock levels.
• Automatic Location Search: Module to suggest optimal locations for stock.
2. Settings
• Multi-client & Multi-language support
• Up to 5 packaging levels per item
• Traceability configuration: Batch, FIFO, etc.
• Third-party configuration: Main supplier concept
• Delivery settings: Days, routes, prioritization of articles and third parties
• Preparation modes configuration: Preparation circuits impacting mission
generation, document creation, and validations
• User & Rights settings
• Printer configuration: Print edits and orientations
3. Receiving
• Simple receipt process
• Reception expectations management
• Quantitative & qualitative control
• Identification & labeling
• Paper or radio frequency-based receipt
• Automatic addressing
Sensitivity: Internal

• Run-of-the-river or cadenced reception
• Receipt generation via interface
• Information exchange: Fax, EDI, email
4. Inventory Management
• Global stock overview: By supplier, quality, condition, etc.
• Stock view by item & by location with filters
• Stock details & movements
• Unit or bulk modification of stock selections
• Movement management: In stock, blocked, expected, in receipt, on order, in
preparation, available
5. Order Preparations
• Order interface management
• Preparation by unit or wave (grouped by recipient, customer, distribution
point, etc.)
• Preparation list generation (mock-up)
• Flow optimization by type
• Order visualization with filters (dates, customers, carriers)
• Replenishment of picking locations
6. Shipment Management
• Document management: Paper or electronic transport documents and
delivery notes (EDI, email)
• Tracking preparation progress: By grouping, tour, or pallet
• Route visualization: Geographical map with deadlines
7. Inventory
• Inventory creation: Choose zones, locations, items

SpeedWMS – Place Management
Purpose:
Define and manage the concept of “Place” for transportation in SpeedWMS,
ensuring loads are correctly characterized and documented.
1. Definition
• Place management is a transport characteristic assigned to a load.
• It is defined in the “Transport” tab of the load in SpeedWMS.
2. Functionality
• Users can create or modify places directly in the table provided within the
module.
• The “Mention” field allows free-text comments that may be used for:
o Documentation purposes
o Transport instructions
3. Key Notes
• Place management ensures proper load classification and can be
referenced in transport documents.
• Changes are effective immediately once saved in the system.
Sensitivity: Internal

SpeedWMS – Manufacturing Configuration
Access:
• Menu: Settings → Manufacturing
Purpose:
• This section provides configuration options directly linked to the
Manufacturing module in SpeedWMS.
• All available options are presented in the Manufacturing section of the
online help.
Key Points:
• Configuration settings here affect how the Manufacturing module operates.
• Only users with access to the Manufacturing module can modify these
settings.
• Settings may include workflows, production rules, and operational
parameters specific to manufacturing processes.
Sensitivity: Internal

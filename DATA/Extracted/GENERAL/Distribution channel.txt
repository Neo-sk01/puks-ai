Distribution Channel
Access:
Settings → Other → Distribution channels
Purpose:
• Enables the distribution of goods across multiple orders.
• Supports distribution via different channels, allowing the warehouse to
manage how items are allocated and shipped to various recipients.
Usage:
• The channels defined here are later used in the Distribution Management
module to guide order fulfillment and allocation.
Sensitivity: Internal

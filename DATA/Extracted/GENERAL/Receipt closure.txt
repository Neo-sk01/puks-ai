SpeedWMS – Receipt Closure
Purpose:
The receipt closure step finalizes a SpeedWMS receipt, freezing its information and
ensuring no further modifications can be made to the receipt lines or media.
1. Scope of Operation
• Closure is the final step in the receipt lifecycle.
• Once closed:
o No new media can be added.
o Existing lines cannot be modified or deleted.
o Receipt information remains accessible in a read-only (advisory)
mode.
o Attached documents can still be viewed and edited.
2. System Integration
• If the system requires feedback to the n+1 system, it typically occurs after
receipt closure.
3. How to Close a Receipt
1. Access the Receipt Management screen.
2. Use either:
o The contextual menu option “Close Receipt”, or
o The Close Receipt button in the SpeedWMS command bar.
3. Confirm the closure action.
4. Notes
• Closure ensures data integrity by preventing accidental modifications to
processed receipts.
• Closed receipts remain available for reporting and audit purposes.

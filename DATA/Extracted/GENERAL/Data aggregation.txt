Data Aggregation
Grouping Function
• Purpose: Allows grouping of table data according to selected criteria.
• Limitation: Can only group numeric fields.
• How to use:
1. Select the field(s) you want to group by.
2. Click on the Sum (fx) button to aggregate the data.
Detail Function
• Purpose: Returns the table to its original, ungrouped state.
• How to use:
1. Select the grouped line you want to expand.
2. Click on the Detail (fx) button to restore the detailed view.
Sensitivity: Internal

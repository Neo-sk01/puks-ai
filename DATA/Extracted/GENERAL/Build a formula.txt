Build a Formula
Purpose
• Allows users to create custom queries or calculations within SpeedWMS.
• Example: calculating stock valuation, generating KPIs, or deriving any metric
using available fields.
Access
• Click on the fx button in the relevant screen or module where formula
creation is supported.
Steps to Build a Formula
1. Open the Formula Builder
o Click on fx to launch the formula construction window.
2. Name the Formula
o Enter a meaningful name at the top of the window (e.g., Stock
Valuation).
3. Select Fields
o Pick the fields you want to use in your calculation.
o Example: Quantity in stock and Unit price.
4. Add Operators
o Use the mathematical operators provided: +, -, *, /, parentheses ().
5. Construct the Formula
o Example formula for stock valuation:
o (Quantity in stock) * (Unit price)
o Steps in the builder:
▪ Open a parenthesis (
▪ Send Quantity in stock to the formula window
▪ Click * (multiplication)
▪ Send Unit price to the formula window
Sensitivity: Internal

[PAGE 2]
▪ Close parenthesis )
6. Validate
o Click Validate to save and make the formula usable.
Tips
• Use parentheses to control calculation order.
• You can combine multiple fields and operators to create complex formulas.
• Once saved, the formula can be used in reports, dashboards, or data queries
within SpeedWMS.
Sensitivity: Internal

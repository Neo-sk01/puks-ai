SpeedWMS – Photo Tab
Purpose:
Enable the association of images with items to improve identification and
visualization within the system.
1. Functionality
• The Photo tab allows users to attach an image to an item sheet.
• To add an image:
1. Click the “Select an image” button.
2. Browse and select the desired image file.
• Supported file types: All standard image formats accepted by SpeedWMS.
2. Use Cases
• Images can be displayed in the stock display screen for each reference.
• Improves identification and verification of items during:
o Receiving
o Picking
o Inventory management
3. Notes
• The image is linked directly to the item and will be displayed wherever item
references appear in the system.
• No additional configuration is required after the image is selected and
saved.
Sensitivity: Internal

Customer Account Management
Purpose
• Allows an activity to be split by customer account, meaning that all receiving
and preparation operations are assigned to a specific customer account.
• Ensures that data, items, and operations can be segregated per customer.
Activation
• Must be enabled in the activity sheet:
o Access: System → Activity → Double-click on the activity
o Option to activate Customer account management.
Key Features
1. Common vs. Specific Data
o Data (items, third parties, etc.) can be set as common for all accounts
or specific per customer.
o Specific data will have their item codes prefixed with the relevant
customer account code.
2. Creating Customer Accounts
o Access via: Settings → Third p. → Customer accounts
o Click Add to open the new customer account form.
o Fields:
▪ Activity: auto-filled, showing the activity in which the operator
is located.
▪ Code: unique reference for the customer account.
▪ Designation: customer account name.
▪ Prefix: alphanumeric (max 5 characters) – used to generate
item codes specific to this account.
▪ Logo: optional, assign a logo to the account.
▪ Coordinates: contact information.
Sensitivity: Internal

[PAGE 2]
▪ Comments: additional info for order preparation, delivery
notes, or billing.
▪ Additional Information: via dedicated tab for further details.
3. System Restart
o After creating or modifying customer accounts, SpeedWMS must be
restarted for changes to take effect.
4. Diverse Operations
o Customer accounts can have specific operations assigned,
configured in the Diverse Operations section.
Summary
• Enables multi-account management within a single activity.
• Ensures data segregation, code prefixing, and account-specific operations.
• Provides flexibility for customer-specific branding, notes, and operational
needs.
Sensitivity: Internal

Storage Validation
Access:
• Storage tab of the receipt.
• Select the lines to be stored.
• Click Validate Storage (button at the bottom or via the contextual menu).
What Happens During Storage Validation
1. The system moves the selected media (pallets, bins, etc.) to their planned
location automatically.
2. The status of the support is updated to:
o 01 – Support in place
o This indicates the media is now stored and available for order
preparation.
3. Information for each line is updated in the system after processing.
Key Points:
• Storage validation finalizes the placement of media in the warehouse.
• Only validated media (status 01) can be used for further operations like order
picking or preparation.

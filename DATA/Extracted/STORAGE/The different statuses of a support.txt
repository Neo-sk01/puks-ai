SpeedWMS – Status of a Support
When stock is generated during reception, each storage support (e.g., pallet, bin) is
assigned a status depending on its handling and location assignment.
Initial Status After Reception
1. 01 – Support in place
o Support is already in its location.
o Typically used when locations are not managed.
2. 02 – Location to be assigned
o Support is on the reception platform.
o Destination location is unknown at this stage.
Assigned Location
• When a destination location is assigned (manually in the Storage tab or via
automatic location search), the support status changes to:
3. 03 – Movement to be performed
o Support knows its planned storage location, but has not yet been
moved.
After Movement
• Once the support is moved to the planned location:
o Status changes from 03 → 01
o Initial location is replaced with the planned location.
o The movement is recorded in Speed history.
Key Notes:
• Status 01 can represent both “already in place” and “moved to its
destination.”
• Status 02 is temporary until the system or user assigns a location.
Sensitivity: Internal

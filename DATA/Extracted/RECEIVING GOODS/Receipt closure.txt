SOP: Receipt Closure in Speed WMS
Sensitivity: Internal
1. Purpose
This SOP describes the receipt closure process in Speed WMS, including its
effects, constraints, and operational usage.
2. Definition of Receipt Closure
Receipt closure is the final step in the lifecycle of a Speed WMS receipt.
The closure operation freezes all receipt data and confirms the reception as
complete.
Once closed, the receipt becomes read-only.
3. Effects of Receipt Closure
After a receipt is closed:
• The receipt cannot be modified
• No new receipt lines can be added
• Existing receipt lines cannot be edited or deleted
• No new media or attachments can be added
However:
• The receipt remains accessible in consultation mode
• All attached documents can still be viewed and printed
• Receipt data can be used for reporting and audit purposes
4. System Integration Considerations
• Receipt closure is generally the trigger point for:
o Data feedback to external systems (n+1 systems)
o ERP or sales management system synchronization
• Only closed receipts should be considered final and reliable for downstream
processing

5. How to Close a Receipt
Receipt closure is performed from the Receipt Management Screen using one of
the following methods:
• Contextual menu → Close Receipt
• Speed WMS command bar → Close Receipt button
Both methods perform the same operation and apply the same validation rules.
6. Preconditions for Closure
Before closing a receipt, ensure that:
• All receipt lines have been correctly recorded
• Quantities and item references are validated
• Required documents or media have been attached
• No further modifications are expected
7. Best Practices
• Close receipts only after full verification
• Avoid premature closure, as reopening is not possible
• Ensure closure is aligned with external system synchronization schedules

Restocking Calculation Method
Purpose:
• To determine the quantity of items to be replenished at a picking location,
taking into account current stock, orders in preparation, and thresholds.
Factors Considered:
1. Replenishment Unit: The unit used for replenishment (e.g., pallet, box).
2. Max Quantity at Picking: Maximum quantity that can be held at the picking
location.
3. Replenishment Threshold: Minimum quantity that triggers a replenishment if
stock falls below it.
4. Quantity in Orders in Preparation: Items already allocated to orders
currently in preparation.
Restocking Logic:
1. Check Orders in Preparation:
o If the item exists in orders in preparation and the current picking
quantity is less than the maximum at picking, a replenishment is
generated.
o Quantity to replenish = Max at picking – Quantity currently at picking
2. Check Replenishment Threshold:
o If the item is not in orders in preparation:
▪ If the current picking quantity < threshold, generate a
replenishment.
▪ If current picking quantity ≥ threshold, no replenishment is
generated.
Calculation Formula:
Need = AvailableQtyPicking – sum(PickingSamplingsNotValidated)
+ sum(REA_NotValidated) – ReplenishmentThreshold
Quantity to Replenish:
Sensitivity: Internal

[PAGE 2]
QtyToReplenish = PicSamplQty + MaxPickingQty – PicAvailableQty
– ReplQty – QtyTransfToPick – QtyTransfFromPick
• Adjustments can be made depending on saturation, adding threshold
quantity or max picking quantity.
Example:
• Picking Unit: Pallet (1 pallet = 15 parcels)
• Picking Capacity: 2 pallets
• Threshold: 10% (3 parcels)
• Orders Launched: 45 parcels
• Current Quantity at Picking: 5 parcels
Calculation:
Quantity to Replenish = 45 + 30 – 5 = 70 parcels
• Optimal quantity to replenish: 70 parcels
• Minimum quantity needed to serve orders: 43 parcels

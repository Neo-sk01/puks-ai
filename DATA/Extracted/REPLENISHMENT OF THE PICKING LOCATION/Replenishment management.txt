Topic: Replenishment Management
Access Path: Preparation / Replenishment
Primary Function: To select and validate the movement of stock to picking
locations.
1. Replenishment Selection
You can initiate a replenishment task using several selection methods:
• Replenishment Window: The primary interface for selection.
• Picking Location: Select directly from a specific picking location that needs
stock.
• Specific Item: Select based on a particular item that requires
replenishment.
Filtering: After selecting a general Site, you can apply additional filters to narrow
the selection further based on:
• The collection site (where stock is taken from).
• The restocking drop-off site (where stock is delivered to).
2. Replenishment Validation
Replenishment tasks can be validated in two ways:
• Partial Validation: Confirm the completion of only a portion of the requested
replenishment quantity.
• Total Validation: Confirm the completion of the entire requested
replenishment quantity.
Note: For detailed procedures and rules governing validation, please refer to the
dedicated system documentation sections for validation.

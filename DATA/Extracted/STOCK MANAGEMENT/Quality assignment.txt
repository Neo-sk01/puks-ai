SpeedWMS – Quality Assignment
Access
• Menu: Stock / Quality allocation
Purpose
• Applies quality rules to stock items according to the configurations defined
in the environment.
• Ensures that stock in the warehouse is assigned the correct quality status
based on pre-configured rules.
Notes
• The quality rules are defined at the item level in the settings.
• This process helps maintain consistency in stock quality and prevents
misallocation during picking or replenishment.
Sensitivity: Internal

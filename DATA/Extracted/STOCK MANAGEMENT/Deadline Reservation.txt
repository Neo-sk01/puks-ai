SpeedWMS – Deadline Reservation
Access
• Menu: Stock / Deadline Reservation
Purpose
• Analyzes reserved stock to ensure that reservations are up-to-date.
• Automatically removes reservations for all stock items whose reservation
date is earlier than the current date.
Notes
• Works in conjunction with the reservation setup at the item level (Unit
modification section of the Item).
• Helps prevent outdated reservations from blocking stock availability.
Sensitivity: Internal
